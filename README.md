## About the Series

**Avatar: The Last Airbender** is an animated fantasy-adventure series that aired on *Nickelodeon* from 2005 to 2008. It was created by Michael Dante DiMartino and Bryan Konietzko.

> A story about friendship, balance, and doing the right thing even when it is hard.

---

## Main Characters

1. **Aang** - the Avatar and the last Airbender
2. **Katara** - a Water Tribe waterbender and healer
3. **Sokka** - the team's strategist and joker
4. **Toph** - an Earthbender who is blind and one of the strongest benders
5. **Zuko** - a Fire Nation prince who becomes an ally

## The Four Nations

- Water Tribe
- Earth Kingdom
- Fire Nation
- Air Nomads
